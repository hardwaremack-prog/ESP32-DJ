ESP32 DJ

Two-deck MP3 DJ mixer for the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3 and V4. You control it from your phone's browser and it plays through an I2S DAC.

This browser can't flash boards. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 · Flash your board

Heltec V3

ESP32-S3, 8 MB flash, no PSRAM. Shorter scratch/echo buffers.

Heltec V4 (recommended)

ESP32-S3, 16 MB flash, 2 MB PSRAM. 3-second ring buffers, stereo echo, bigger library.

Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable, click Install, and pick the serial port. If no port shows up, hold PRG, tap RST, then release PRG. Later updates can be installed over Wi-Fi from Settings → Firmware update.

Every pin can be changed in Settings. The ESP32-S3 can't stream Bluetooth audio itself because it has no Classic Bluetooth, so use a small transmitter module.

3 · Play

  1. Format a microSD card as FAT32 and copy MP3s into a music folder. You can also upload them later from the browser.
  2. Power the board. The OLED shows a hotspot named ESP32-DJ-XXXX (password djmix1234).
  3. Join it from your phone and open http://192.168.4.1. On your home Wi-Fi it's http://esp32dj.local.
  4. Open Library, load a track on A and one on B, and mix. Turn on Auto-DJ to let it mix for you.

OLED button: a short press changes the screen (decks / network / system). Hold it to toggle Auto-DJ.
